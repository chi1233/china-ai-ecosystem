Research Insights

Methodology and sources

Dataset purpose

The China AI Ecosystem Table is a curated intelligence and research dataset. It maps selected firms, public companies, platform providers, research institutions, capital actors, and infrastructure anchors relevant to China's AI ecosystem.

Selection

Inclusion is based on analytical relevance, public evidence availability, and ecosystem role. The dataset is neither a ranking nor a complete census. It includes private firms, listed firms, business units, products, research organizations, and capital providers, which are identified by entity type.

This is a curated ecosystem dataset, not a market ranking, investment recommendation, comprehensive national census, or causal estimate of policy effects. Inclusion reflects analytical relevance and evidence availability. Geographic concentration in this sample does not by itself establish that policy designation caused ecosystem outcomes.

Evidence

Core fields are classified as disclosed, reported, estimated, inferred, or unverified. Primary sources include regulatory filings, prospectuses, stock-exchange documents, government documents, company disclosures, and institutional records. Corroborated reporting from outlets such as Reuters, Bloomberg, Caixin, Financial Times, Nikkei, CNBC, SCMP, TechNode, 36Kr, Pandaily, and specialist sources may be used for private-market or emerging ecosystem information when primary records are unavailable.

Disclosed
Primary source, filing, exchange disclosure, government document, or official institutional statement.
Reported
Credible financial or specialist reporting, shown with source name and date.
Estimated
Third-party estimate, avoided for headline numbers.
Inferred
Analytical interpretation based on documented facts, never displayed as direct fact.
Unverified
Held out of the public fact layer.

Financial data schema

The former combined funding and scale field has been removed because it merged incompatible categories. Three separate fields replace it, and a value appears only with a type, a date or reporting period, a source, and a claim status.

Capital event
Completed or credibly reported financing, IPO, placement, or acquisition.
Public-market value
Market capitalization for a listed company or listed parent only, with ticker and exact close date.
Operating scale
Revenue, customers, users, shipments, deployment, or another defined operating metric with its reporting period.

An intended valuation, fundraising target, press rumor, or parent-company market capitalization is never presented as the funding or valuation of a subsidiary, product, or business unit.

Interpretation

The developmental-orchestration framework is an analytical lens. A domain label identifies a documented or hypothesized ecosystem mechanism. It does not by itself establish that a policy, investor, university, or infrastructure provider caused a company's outcome.

Case design

Beijing is the capstone's primary case. Hangzhou is a contrastive reference. Shanghai, Shenzhen, Hefei, Wuhan, Guangzhou, and national-level entities provide broader ecosystem context. This visualization does not provide a matched comparative design or causal estimate of city policy effects.

Relationship taxonomy

A network edge identifies an exact relationship type. An edge is never drawn from geographic proximity, founder education, general policy relevance, or narrative plausibility alone. Founder education is never represented as a formal spinout or technology-transfer relationship.

  • Financing
  • Formal spinout
  • Founder education
  • Formal university or research partnership
  • Joint laboratory
  • Public investment or guidance-fund investment
  • Public procurement or deployment
  • Cloud, compute, or model dependency
  • Research institution anchor
  • Acquisition

Updates

Research evidence is frozen as of 17 September 2026 for this release. Dynamic news signals are maintained separately and are not evidence for the table's core factual classifications.
